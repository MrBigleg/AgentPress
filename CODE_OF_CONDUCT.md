# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in AgentPress a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and empathetic.
- Give and accept feedback gracefully.
- Focus discussion on the project's goals and the community's well-being.
- Respect differing viewpoints and experiences.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discriminatory language or imagery, personal attacks, trolling, threats, unwanted sexual attention, deliberate intimidation, and publishing another person's private information are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. Conduct concerns should be reported privately through the repository owner's GitHub profile. Maintainers will review reports promptly, fairly, and confidentially.
